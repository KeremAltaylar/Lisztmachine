# Surge patches carried with Lisztmachine

Surge XT patches (GPL-3.0, from the Surge XT distribution and its third-party patch collections, credited to
their authors by folder) that the songs use but some Surge releases do not ship (Kerem's Mac, 2026-11-14: 15 missing).
The engine looks here when Surge's own patch folders lack a patch (`findSurgePatch`, src/engine/Engine.cpp).
