# Lisztmachine sound palette: Chorale scene (four voices, IDM rhythm).
# Tracks: soprano alto tenor bass (the voices, each its own track and echo), sub, drums,
# micro, blips, piano. "voices" puts a patch on all four voice tracks; they change sound together.
#
# The voices play in pure tuning (the engine strips unison detune, drift and chorus-type
# effects). Chosen by measuring a held note's beating with that applied (a sine reads 1 %):
# Clarinet 2-9 %, Flute 2-6 %, Sunday 7-15 %, Organ 2 7-12 %, Retro Choir 8-13 %, Still 8-14 %,
# Comb Strings 9-23 %. Left out: Ooh (35 %, a built-in tremolo), Pad 1 (43 %), Tragic Winds (33 cents
# sharp and a +11 dBFS transient), Synth Choir (pitch jumps by octaves), Piano Remains (pure
# tuning puts them an octave off; the piano track is not purified).
#
# part   gain   bright  patch
#   gain     dB for this patch: from the mean of two held notes (40 and 64), to -22 dB, minus 8 for four voices
#   bright   spectral centroid in Hz, measured (orders dark -> bright over the piece)
voices  -11.1    300  Keys/Organ 2
voices    0.0    400  Pads/Still
voices  -12.6    500  Winds/Flute 1
voices   -0.2   1000  Pads/Retro Choir
voices  -11.0   1350  Winds/Clarinet
voices   -3.6   1500  Pads/Sunday
voices   -4.1   2700  Pads/Subtle Comb Strings

# In-mix corrections from seed 1, per section (voices / guitar+drums, dB RMS): Clarinet in the Theme
# -23.2 under Uni Saw FB -18.7, Flute in the Breakdown -18.1: Clarinet +3, Flute -2, Uni Saw FB -5.
# Balance between the voices (dB), set from their loudness in a full render.
# Measured in seed 1 before: soprano -28.2, alto -29.1, tenor -30.5, bass -33.1 dB RMS.

# Each voice its own echo: time in beats, feedback (0-0.95), mix. Different divisions per voice
# make the tails interleave: soprano a dotted eighth, alto a quarter, tenor a dotted quarter,
# bass a half note. The renderer's --feedback sets every feedback at once.
delay soprano 0.75 0.45 0.28
delay alto    1.0  0.40 0.22
delay tenor   1.5  0.38 0.20
