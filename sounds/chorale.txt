# micro, blips, piano. "voices" puts a patch on all four voice tracks; they change sound together.
#
# The voices play in pure tuning (the engine strips unison detune, drift and chorus-type
# effects). Chosen by measuring a held note's beating with that applied (a sine reads 1 %):
# Clarinet 2-9 %, Flute 2-6 %, Sunday 7-15 %, Organ 2 7-12 %, Retro Choir 8-13 %, Still 8-14 %,
# Comb Strings 9-23 %. 2026-10-30: Clarinet out (Kerem: "fix the detuned high-pitch synth"): even
# pure it runs sharp as it climbs, +4 cents at C4, +33 at C5, +94 at G5, an octave off at C6.
# Flute 1 out too: 19 cents sharp at C5 and C6 (against the detector's own offset, which every patch shares). Left out: Ooh (35 %, a built-in tremolo), Pad 1 (43 %), Tragic Winds (33 cents
# sharp and a +11 dBFS transient), Synth Choir (pitch jumps by octaves), Piano Remains (pure
# tuning puts them an octave off; the piano track is not purified).

# part   gain   bright  patch
