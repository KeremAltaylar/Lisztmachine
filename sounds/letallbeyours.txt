# Lisztmachine sound palette: Let All Be Yours (drony acid techno).
# Tracks: drone kick bass acid stab hats clap sub fx.
#
# Chosen by measuring a held note with pure tuning (--audition ... --pure): pitch against the
# note and beating (a sine reads 1 %). Left out: Acid Seq 1-3, Acidofil, Evilish, Gate Chord,
# Burial Ground (they sequence their own pitches, out of the key), Deep End (96 cents flat),
# Distorted MW (14 cents flat), Burden (19 cents flat), Endgame (70 %), Death To Gator (56 %),
# Eighties Drone (39 %), Squelch, Resofest 2 (they sound an octave down, unlike the other leads).
#
# part   gain   bright  patch
#   gain     dB: from a held note's RMS, to -22 dB (chords minus 5-6)
#   bright   spectral centroid in Hz, measured (orders dark -> bright over the piece)

# The drone: root, fifth, octave (and a high third when driven). Semihaunt 8 %, Yeti Funeral 11 %. (Ghost Pad left out: 14 dB quieter in the mix at the peak.)
drone    -0.4    373  Pads/Semihaunt
drone    -2.5    296  Pads/Yeti Funeral

# The kick, hats and clap: the engine's own, sample-exact, the kick tuned to the tonic.
kick      0.0     60  internal/kick
hats      0.0   9000  internal/hat
clap      0.0   2000  internal/clap
sub      -6.0     40  internal/sub

# The rolling bass (these sound an octave below the written note; the score is written up).
# Sub 2 6 %, Rubber Bass 6 %, Digibass 8 %, Rumble 12 %, Saw Lo-Fi 8 %.
bass     -8.8    129  Basses/Sub 2
bass     -9.0    141  Basses/Digibass
bass    -10.6    163  Basses/Rumble
bass    -12.2    824  Basses/Rubber Bass
bass     -8.6   2574  Basses/Saw Lo-Fi

# The acid line, at the written pitch: Sine-Saw Acidish 3 %, Classic Lead 1 5 %, Moogy Saw 8 %,
# Sharpish 3 %, Harsher 3 %. Its filter is swept by the score.
acid     -6.5    302  Leads/Sine-Saw Acidish
acid    -10.0    187  Leads/Classic Lead 1
acid      1.4   1020  Leads/Moogy Saw
acid     -6.1   1223  Leads/Sharpish
acid     -3.6   4472  Leads/Harsher

# The stabs, three or four notes: Serious Distortion 11 % (an octave down), Notched Saws 5 %, Saw Pluck 1 %.
stab     -3.2   1204  Polysynths/Serious Distortion
stab     -0.2   4994  Polysynths/Notched Saws
stab     -4.9   4040  Plucks/Saw Pluck

# The riser and the dark air: Unsettler 6 %, Radio Noise (noise).
fx       -0.8    217  FX/Unsettler
fx        4.0   4180  FX/Radio Noise

# Echoes: the acid and the stabs a dotted eighth, ping-pong against the kick.
delay acid    0.75 0.35 0.18
delay stab    0.75 0.45 0.25
delay clap    1.5  0.25 0.10

# Balance, from solo renders of seed 1 per section (dB RMS while playing): kick -18.5, bass -24,
# acid -25, stab -25..-29, hats -26, clap -30, sub -21, fx -19 in the Rise. Kick first, then
# bass and acid, the stabs and claps under them.
# and 4 dB down for all, so the master limiter does not squash the kick (it took -3.8 dB at the peak).
gain drone -4.0
gain kick  -4.0
gain bass  -2.0
gain acid  -1.0
gain stab  -3.0
gain hats  -4.0
gain clap   1.0
gain sub   -7.0
gain fx    -8.0
master 9.0
